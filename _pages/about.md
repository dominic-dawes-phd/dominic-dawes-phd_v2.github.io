My goal is to build algorithms and systems for autonomous robots and embodied agents that reason about and interact with the real world. My research lies at the intersection of collaborative robotics, machine learning, and computer vision. I focus on developing methods and principles of perception, decision-making, and life-long learning to deploy general-purpose robot autonomy in the wild.
